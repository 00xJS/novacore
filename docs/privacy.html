GALAXY ROYALE
PRIVACY POLICY

We don't collect your data

Galaxy Royale is a single-player game that runs on your iPhone. It has no accounts, no servers, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. This page explains what the game keeps, where, and the few features that talk to anyone else.

What the game stores

  • Your save lives on your device.
  • A backup of it goes to your own iCloud account (iCloud key-value storage), if iCloud is on. Apple keeps it for you; we never receive it.
  • The home-screen widget and the raid Live Activity read a small snapshot the game writes on your device (timers, resources, might). It never leaves your phone.
  • Notifications are scheduled on your device. Nothing is sent anywhere.

Optional features

  • Game Center is off until you turn it on in Settings. Then your scores and achievements go to Apple under your Game Center account. We don't collect them.
  • Report a problem opens the iOS share sheet with a text report you can read first. Nothing is sent unless you choose where to share it.

In-app purchases

Dark Matter can be bought in the ITEMS shop. Purchases go through Apple's App Store: Apple handles the payment and your payment details, and we never see them. The game only learns that a purchase went through, so it can add the Dark Matter to your save on your device.

What we collect

Nothing. The game contains no analytics, advertising or crash-reporting code, and Unity's hardware statistics are switched off.

Children

The game collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If a future version changes any of this, this page will say so before that version ships.
