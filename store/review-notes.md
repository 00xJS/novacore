# Notes for App Review — draft

---

Galaxy Royale is a single-player, offline strategy game. There is no login and no account, and the game plays without a connection; only buying Dark Matter needs one.

New games start with a short training that walks through the basics (it can be skipped, and restarted from Settings › Help).

To see every system quickly: on first launch choose NEW GAME › TESTING. That starts with a full resource and Dark Matter kit, speed-ups and free warps, so nothing is gated by waiting. STANDARD is the normal start.

Things worth a look:
- The base screen: upgrade buildings; the queues button shows timers.
- MAP: 249 simulated rival commanders. Tap a planet to raid or spy; tap the centre for the Galactic Core.
- MORE: clan, the Pirate Dreadnought (when one is in the galaxy), the market, events, rankings and awards.
- The CITADEL tab (or tip the planet north): five buildings, including the Academy (COMMANDER LEADS on the fleet screens) and the Trade Consulate.
- MORE › EXPLORE: expeditions; send a fleet, and halfway through a choice appears.
- MORE › EVENTS: the galaxy event now running; several (a comet, a caravan, a storm, a supernova) put something on the map, and SHOW ON MAP flies there.
- MORE › STORY: the campaign, The Long Night. In a TESTING game, chapter 1 opens as soon as the Command Center reaches level 5 (in STANDARD it also waits two galaxy days). Each chapter ends with a Pirate Lord whose lair is marked on the map (SHOW THE LAIR).
- MORE › PROJECTS (from Command Center 15) and MORE › REFITS: mega-projects and ship modules.
- Profile › CODEX & COLLECTIONS: everything met so far, with earn-only planet skins.
- EVENTS & SEASON also shows the week's galaxy twist and, around certain dates, a seasonal festival (it follows the device's calendar).
- Profile › ALL SETTINGS: music, text size, colour-blind colours, reduced motion, notifications, Game Center (optional), Help, Report a problem, and under ABOUT the privacy policy, support page and credits.

In-app purchases: "Dark Matter" is the game's premium currency. It's earned by playing (quests, achievements, events, the Galactic Core) and can also be bought in six consumable packs: ITEMS (bottom bar) › DARK MATTER tab. Prices come from the App Store. Each purchase is credited once, saved, and only then finished, so an interrupted purchase is delivered at the next launch. There's nothing to restore: consumables live in the save, which backs up to the player's iCloud. The Planetary Resurfacing item (SHOP › SKINS) gives a random cosmetic surface; its description states the odds.
