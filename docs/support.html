SUPPORT

Need a hand, Commander?

Report a problem

In the game, tap your commander at the top left, then ALL SETTINGS › REPORT A PROBLEM. It writes a short report (your device, the game's version and recent errors, never your name or your save) that you read first, then share by mail or any app you like.

Send it to: josephsuckling@live.com

Purchases

  • Dark Matter bought in the ITEMS shop is added to your save as soon as Apple confirms the payment. If the game closes mid-purchase, it adds it the next time it opens.
  • Dark Matter is used up as you spend it, so there's nothing to restore on a new phone: it travels inside your save, with the iCloud backup.
  • Charged but no Dark Matter? Email josephsuckling@live.com. For a refund, use Apple's reportaproblem.apple.com.

Your save

  • The game saves on your iPhone as you play and backs up to your iCloud when iCloud is on for your Apple ID. Your commander profile shows when it last backed up.
  • New phone? Sign in to the same Apple ID and install the game: its title screen offers RESTORE FROM ICLOUD.
  • Starting over: your commander profile › RESET EMPIRE. It asks twice, because there's no undo.

Common questions

Does the galaxy keep going while I'm away? Yes. When you come back, the game fast-forwards the whole galaxy through the time you missed and tells you what happened.

Do I need an internet connection? No. Everything runs on your phone. Only buying Dark Matter needs a connection, to reach the App Store.

Are there ads or in-app purchases? No ads. Nothing to buy in this version.
