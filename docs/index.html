GALAXY ROYALE
A LIVING GALAXY OF 249 RIVALS

Build a colony. Rule the galaxy.

Galaxy Royale is space strategy for iPhone. You build a colony on a planet you can spin in your hand, chart the wild land to its south, raise a fleet from 23 hulls, and hold your own against 249 rival commanders who play by the same rules you do. They build, research, form clans and raid each other, and you, while you're away.

In the game

  • Your colony on a globe: Command, the Mining Belt, the Frontier, the Spaceport, and the Wilds under survey fog.
  • Raids, pirate camps, spy probes, and a replay of every battle.
  • Clans of up to 15, joint strikes, the Galactic Core and the Pirate Dreadnought.
  • Single-player and offline: no account, no ads, no tracking.
